Bars are split by |: Dm7 G7 | Cmaj7 is a bar of two chords, then a bar of one. A line with no | gives each chord its own bar, so a pasted chord sheet works; chords with beats, like Dm7:2 G7:2, share a bar. [Verse] on its own line starts a section. Chord types: C Cm C7 Cm7 Cmaj7 C6 Cm6 C9 C13 C7b9 C7#9 Calt Csus4 Cm7b5 Cdim, and slash chords like D/F#. % repeats a bar, N.C. is skipped, and G7:1 sets a chord’s beats. The key is the first chord’s root; the Key menu transposes from there.
